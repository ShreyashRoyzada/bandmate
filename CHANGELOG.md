# Changelog

Bandmate uses MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH version numbers: PATCH for bug fixes, MINOR for new features, MAJOR when something has to be set up again.

## 1.0.0 (first public release)

**Today**
- A plan for the day in three lines: how hard to train, protein and calories left, when to go to bed.
- Sleep with stages and split nights joined, sleep debt over the week, heart rate through the day, resting heart rate.

**Food**
- Log a meal from up to 4 photos, a few words or your voice; the AI estimates protein, calories, carbs and fat.
- One-tap meals, a remembered mess menu, and meals added to earlier days.

**Workout**
- Every session from your watch, with effort from heart rate, plus sessions spotted from heart rate alone.
- Weights and reps for each session.
- Missing sessions added from a screenshot of the Health app's workout list, or by telling the coach.

**Faces**
- Two built-in packs: Digital Classic (11 faces) and Dot Theme (5). Any .f3pack file adds more.
- Sending to the watch from the phone, with steps that tick themselves off; the watch is found again by itself for the next face.

**Progress**
- Where your body is heading from weight, waist, lifts and food, with an animated guide for measuring neck and waist.

**Ask**
- A coach that answers from your data and logs what you tell it you ate or trained, with an Undo.
- Talk with a mic that keeps listening until you tap stop.

**Getting started**
- A setup checklist that ticks itself off, and a short tour of each tab.
- A free Gemini key is optional; Flash Lite is the default model.
